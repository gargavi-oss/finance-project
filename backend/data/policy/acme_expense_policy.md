# ACME India — Corporate & SME Expense Policy (FY26-27)

This policy governs all employee reimbursements, vendor invoices, and tax bills
submitted through the DocForensic AI compliance and verification pipeline in India.

## [EXP-001] Approval thresholds

All expense submissions under **₹10,000 INR** are eligible for automatic approval
provided they are accompanied by a valid GST tax invoice and fall within the categories
listed in [EXP-005]. Submissions between **₹10,000 and ₹1,00,000 INR** require manager
review. Submissions **above ₹1,00,000 INR** require director / CXO sign-off and must be
filed within 14 calendar days of the expense date.

## [EXP-002] GST Tax Invoice & ITC requirements

A valid GST tax invoice with supplier GSTIN is mandatory for every reimbursable
expense of **₹500 INR or more** to ensure Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility under
Section 16 of the CGST Act. Invoices must show a clear breakdown of CGST, SGST,
or IGST with applicable HSN/SAC codes. Scanned receipts must be at minimum 200 DPI
and free of digital tampering artifacts. Handwritten vouchers are accepted only for
amounts under ₹500 INR. Credit-card statements are not accepted as substitutes.

## [EXP-003] Vendor PAN & GSTIN registration

All domestic vendors must provide a verified 15-character Goods & Services Tax
Identification Number (GSTIN), 10-character Permanent Account Number (PAN), and
valid Indian bank account details with an 11-character IFSC code. Ad-hoc vendors
require one-time compliance verification before payment release.

## [EXP-004] Duplicate submissions & shell syndicates

Each invoice may be submitted exactly once. Duplicate submissions — including
re-submissions under a cloned invoice template, alternate vendor trade names, or
sharing identical bank IFSC and account details — are treated as suspected fraud
and routed to the Ring-Detection agent.

## [EXP-005] Eligible expense categories

The following categories are eligible for reimbursement: software & cloud SaaS
subscriptions (subject to 18% GST), office supplies, professional & legal services,
corporate travel & lodging (with GST receipt), training & certifications, and
client entertainment (capped monthly).

## [EXP-006] Currency & settlement

All domestic submissions and vendor settlements must be denominated in Indian
Rupee (**INR / ₹**). Foreign currency expenses must be converted using the RBI
reference exchange rate on the date of transaction.

## [EXP-007] Submission deadlines

Reimbursement requests must be filed within **30 calendar days** of the expense
date to adhere to monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B tax filing cycles. Requests older
than 90 days are automatically rejected.

## [EXP-008] Round-number anomalies

Invoice totals that are perfect round numbers (e.g. exactly ₹50,000.00 or
₹1,00,000.00) on otherwise itemised commercial invoices are flagged for forensic
scrutiny. Round-number fabrication is an established indicator of fraudulent billing.

## [EXP-009] Weekend submissions

Expenses and invoices dated or submitted on weekends (Saturdays or Sundays) are
prohibited from automatic approval and require written business justification and
managerial review.

## [EXP-010] Threshold spikes

Submissions with significant amount spikes above standard departmental limits
(above **₹50,000 INR**) must undergo secondary fraud and threshold compliance
review before payment release.

## [EXP-011] Statutory TDS compliance (Income Tax Act)

Invoices for professional, technical, or contractual services exceeding **₹30,000 INR**
for a single invoice or **₹1,00,000 INR** aggregate per fiscal year are subject to
Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194C or Section 194J of the Indian
Income Tax Act. Reviewers must confirm TDS compliance before processing payment.
