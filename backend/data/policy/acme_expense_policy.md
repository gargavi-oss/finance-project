# ACME Corp — Expense Policy (FY26)

This policy applies to all employee reimbursements, vendor invoices, and receipts
submitted through the DocForensic AI review pipeline.

## [EXP-001] Approval thresholds

All expense submissions under **$500 USD** are eligible for automatic approval
provided they are accompanied by a valid receipt and fall within the categories
listed in [EXP-005]. Submissions between **$500 and $5,000** require manager
review. Submissions **above $5,000** require director sign-off and must be
filed within 14 calendar days of the expense date.

## [EXP-002] Receipt requirements

A receipt is required for every reimbursable expense of **$25 USD or more**.
Scanned receipts must be at minimum 200 DPI and free of visible editing. Hand-
written receipts are accepted only for amounts under $25. Credit-card statements
are not accepted as substitutes for original receipts.

## [EXP-003] Vendor approval

Only vendors on the **Approved Vendor List** are eligible for reimbursement.
Ad-hoc vendors require a one-time approval ticket filed with the finance team
prior to submission. Vendors with more than **10 prior submissions** in the
last 90 days are eligible for preferred-vendor pricing.

## [EXP-004] Duplicate submissions

Each invoice may be submitted exactly once. Duplicate submissions — including
re-submissions under a different vendor name, a different invoice number, or a
slightly modified total — are treated as suspected fraud and routed to the
Ring-Detection agent for cross-document fingerprint analysis.

## [EXP-005] Eligible expense categories

The following categories are eligible for reimbursement: software subscriptions,
business meals (per-diem limits apply), office supplies, professional services,
travel (with pre-approval), training & conferences (with pre-approval), and
client entertainment (capped monthly).

## [EXP-006] Currency & conversion

All submissions must use the local currency of the expense. Foreign-currency
receipts must be converted using the daily mid-market rate published by the
company's finance portal on the date of submission.

## [EXP-007] Submission deadlines

Reimbursement requests must be filed within **30 calendar days** of the expense
date. Requests older than 90 days are automatically rejected.

## [EXP-008] Round-number anomalies

Invoice totals that are perfect round numbers (e.g. exactly $1,000.00 or
$2,500.00) on otherwise itemised invoices are flagged for additional review.
Round-number fabrication is one of the most common document-fraud patterns.

## [EXP-009] Weekend submissions

Expenses and invoices dated or submitted on weekends (Saturdays or Sundays) are prohibited from automatic approval and require written business justification and managerial review.

## [EXP-010] Threshold spikes

Submissions with significant dollar spikes above standard departmental limits (above $2,500 USD) must undergo secondary fraud and threshold compliance review before payment release.
